# TOEFL iBT 2026 New Format (1-6 Scale)

## Scoring System (Launched Jan 21, 2026)

- **Scale**: 1–6, in 0.5-point increments (like IELTS bands)
- **4 sections**: Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking — each scored 1–6
- **Overall score** = average of 4 section scores, rounded to nearest half band
  - Example: 5.125 → 5.0; 5.25 → 5.5
- **2-year transition**: old 0–120 scale also provided alongside

## Band 5 Proficiency Descriptors (Target Level)

**Reading (B2-C1):**
- Understand a broad range of long and complex texts
- Use morphological and syntactic cues to infer meaning
- Understand informal language including idiomatic expressions
- Identify writer's purpose, supporting details, and make inferences
- Connect information across a text

**Listening (B2-C1):**
- Understand detailed spoken input on concrete and abstract topics at natural pace
- Follow extended conversations and presentations
- Recognize rhetorical structures and interpret idiomatic/colloquial expressions
- Infer speaker attitudes, intentions, and implied meaning

**Writing (B2-C1):**
- Write clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects
- Present arguments and support points of view
- Produce well-structured, coherent text expressing viewpoints at length
- Can use complex grammatical structures appropriately

**Speaking (B2-C1):**
- Respond to questions with clear, coherent elaboration
- Use accurate grammar, varied vocabulary, and intelligible prosody
- Produce text that is well-organized and coherent
- Can use a broad range of complex grammatical structures

## Test Structure (2026 Format)

| Section | Task Types | Items | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| **Reading** | Complete the Words (B1-C1+), Read in Daily Life (A1-C1), Read an Academic Passage (B1-C2) | 50 total | 18-21 min |
| **Listening** | Listen and Choose a Response (A1-B2), Listen to a Conversation (A2-C1), Listen to an Announcement (A2-C1), Listen to an Academic Talk (A2-C2) | 47 total | 18 min |
| **Writing** | Build a Sentence (A1-C2), Write an Email (B1-C2), Write for an Academic Discussion (B1-C2) | 12 total | 23 min |
| **Speaking** | Listen and Repeat (A1-C2), Take an Interview (A1-C2) | 11 total | 8 min |
| **Total** | | 120 items | ~83-89 min |

Key differences from old TOEFL:
- **Adaptive** Reading and Listening (2-stage)
- New task types: "Complete the Words", "Listen and Choose a Response", "Build a Sentence"
- "Listen and Repeat" in Speaking section (pronunciation assessment)
- Shorter overall time (~1.5 hours vs old 2+ hours)

## Tutoring Constraints

- **Listening audio**: ETS uses AI-generated audio + voice actors. For tutoring, we can only provide transcripts and text-based practice; real audio practice requires TPO materials or external resources
- **Speaking evaluation**: Children need to record themselves; AI can evaluate transcripts but not pronunciation/fluency without audio
- **Best approach**: Focus on Reading + Writing + Listening (transcript-based), supplement Speaking with structured response templates

## Tutoring Design Patterns

### 30-min Daily Session Structure

For learners already at B2+ level (spent time abroad), sessions should be:
1. **5 min**: Quick diagnostic (2-3 targeted questions from previous session's weak spots)
2. **20 min**: New material — one skill focus (Reading/Writing/Listening/Speaking)
3. **5 min**: Recap + self-assessment prompt

### Skill Rotation (2-month plan)

| Weeks | Focus | Rationale |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1-2 | Diagnostic + Vocabulary | Identify precise gaps; academic vocab expansion |
| 3-5 | Deep skill work | Rotate Reading→Listening→Writing→Speaking daily |
| 6-8 | Mock tests + Weak-spot drilling | Full TOEFL simulation, then target lowest section |

### Band-Specific Focus

**Band 5 → 5.5 (B2→C1):**
- Reading: Inference questions, author's tone, complex academic passages
- Listening: Note-taking speed, implied meaning, idiomatic expressions
- Writing: Argument sophistication, varied sentence structures, academic register
- Speaking: Spontaneous elaboration, complex grammar, natural prosody

**Band 4.5 → 5 (B2 boundary→solid B2):**
- Reading: Academic vocabulary, paragraph structure, main idea identification
- Writing: Coherent paragraph organization, supporting evidence, transition phrases
- Listening: Detail retention, conversation inference, academic vocabulary in context
- Speaking: Fluency under time pressure, grammar accuracy, vocabulary range
