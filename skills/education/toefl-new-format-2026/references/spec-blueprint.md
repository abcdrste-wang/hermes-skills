## Notes
- Non-scored items in Reading and Listening for future item performance data
- Reading + Listening are 2-stage adaptive tests
- Writing + Speaking are linear (all test takers see same items)

## Text Difficulty (Reading)
- Medium: 800-1100 TextEvaluator (~Grade 10-11), Flesch-Kincaid 7.4-10.5
- High: 1100-1360+ (~Grade 11-12+), FK 10.5-20.0
- Easier texts: 15-50 words; Complex passages: up to 200 words

## Audio Difficulty (Listening)
- Short utterances: ≤6 stressed syllables
- Intermediate: 35-100 words, may include multiple speakers
- Extended monologic: up to 250 words
- AI-generated audio + human voice actors (US/Canadian, Australian, British accents)
- Topics: everyday (directions, schedules, social) + academic (lectures, presentations)
